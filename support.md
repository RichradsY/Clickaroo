## Common questions

**How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone?**
Open Settings in Clickaroo and tap **Restore Purchase**. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. Pro is a one-time purchase, so you never pay again.

**Will I lose my training records if I change phones?**
Your records stay on your device. Before switching phones, go to Settings, tap **Export Backup** and save the file to iCloud Drive or the Files app. On the new phone, tap **Import Backup**. Your regular iPhone backup also includes Clickaroo's data.

**The click is too quiet.**
Turn up the media volume with the side buttons. Clickaroo plays even when your iPhone is in silent mode. A thick case can muffle the speaker. Try the "Sharp" sound outdoors.

**Is the whistle ultrasonic?**
No. iPhone speakers cannot reliably play ultrasonic sound, so the whistle uses tones that both you and your dog can hear. The adjustable pitch lets you pick a sound your dog notices.

**My dog shows aggression, fear or severe anxiety.**
Please work with a qualified positive-reinforcement trainer or a veterinary behaviorist. Clickaroo teaches everyday skills and tricks. It is not a treatment for behavior problems.
