# Clickaroo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026
Developer: Shengxing YANG

Clickaroo is a dog clicker and training app. It is built so that your data stays on your iPhone.

## The short version

- We do not collect any personal data.
- There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- Everything you enter (dog profiles, photos, training sessions and progress) is stored only on your device.

## Data stored on your device

Clickaroo stores the following on your iPhone so the app can work:

- Dog profiles: name, optional birthday and an optional photo you pick from your photo library.
- Training records: sessions, click counts, success marks and skill progress.
- Settings: sound, whistle and haptic preferences.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data. Clickaroo uses the system photo picker, so it can only see the photo you choose. It never sees the rest of your library.

## Backups you create

You can export a backup file and import it again. The file is saved where you choose, for example in the Files app or in iCloud Drive. Clickaroo does not upload it anywhere. Your own iCloud or device backups follow Apple's policies.

## Purchases

Clickaroo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account.

## Children

Clickaroo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. As stated above, it collects no personal data from any user.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
